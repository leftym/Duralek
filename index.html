Games made with care

Duralek is a small game studio. I make the kinds of games I love to play: strategy, sports and story games with a real sense of place.

The name comes from my father. A long time ago, on the other side of the world, he started a company called Duralek. I carry the name on in his honor, and every game I make goes out under it.
