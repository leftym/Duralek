From the Block release notes

Every update, newest first. Serbian lines follow each entry.

1.0 (build 67)

Google Play closed testing, live 2026-11-12.

  • Fast-forward no longer stops on every message from your best friend.
  • You choose how a fast-forward plays and get a short summary at the end. Skipped weeks no longer make your big story choices for you.
  • Faster start: the menu and Continue work right away, with a short three-card tour you can ignore.
  • Fewer English words in Serbian games, with grammar and number fixes.
  • Quick-choice hints now read Likely, About even or Unlikely.
  • Fixed: a double tap on a quick choice still shows the result, training no longer picks a skill that is already at 99, and a few text slips are gone.

Srpski

  • Preskakanje više ne staje na svaku poruku najboljeg druga.
  • Biraš kako preskakanje nedelja igra i na kraju dobijaš kratak pregled. Velike životne odluke se tokom preskakanja ne donose umesto tebe.
  • Brži početak: meni i nastavak karijere rade odmah, uz kratak vodič od tri kartice koji možeš da preskočiš.
  • Manje engleskih reči u srpskoj igri, uz ispravke gramatike i brojeva.
  • Ispravljeno: dvostruki dodir na brzi izbor i dalje prikazuje ishod, a trening više ne bira veštinu koja je već na 99.

Google Play closed testing, live 2026-11-09, 178 countries.

  • Plan six blocks of time each week and see what the plan costs before you play.
  • Play key moments on a real court, or use quick choices.
  • Scouts, offers and a big choice: college in the USA, Europe, or stay home.
  • A 20-season career with a legacy that carries into your next life.
  • Free, offline, no ads, no purchases. English and Serbian.
