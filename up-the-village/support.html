Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome. Write to hello@duralek.games and say which game and phone you use. If something broke, tell us what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Where is my game saved? On your phone, inside the app. There are no accounts and nothing is stored online. Deleting the app or clearing its data deletes the save.

Does the game need the internet? No. It works offline.

Does it collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
