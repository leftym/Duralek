Aliʻi: Rise of a Chief release notes

Every update, newest first.

What came in this release (store version 5.5.22, everything since the last store release 5.4.2)

Versions 5.5.0 to 5.5.21 reach the stores together, as 5.5.22: the stores go from 5.4.2 straight to 5.5.22. Everything new since 5.4.2, checked against the specs, QA reports and reviews; the last fixes are in the 5.5.22 entry below.

  • History and sources: a second history pass against Kamakau and Kuykendall, a "Where the books differ" panel, and nine books on the Sources page (5.5.0, 5.5.1, 5.5.21).
  • New stories: ʻUmi's road, Kahekili's route and the siege of Kaʻuiki, Kamehameha's route (Kekūhaupiʻo, Olowalu, John Young and Isaac Davis, Kapoukahi and Puʻukoholā), Lonoikamakahiki at kōnane, Kauaʻi's places, salt, taro and kapu lines, Kamalālāwalu's Maui campaign, and six folklore cards that have a follow-up 8 to 16 seasons later.
  • Court and family: a Court tab, advisers who remember whether you took their counsel, a Dynasty page, hānai pacts listed, and no hānai demand when you have no child.
  • War: odds and expected losses before you march, Hold and March home on arrival, a warning when your scouting is old, a levy that waits until you march, a prompt before arming takes weapons from another band, and a stronger chief may press a childless chief for Kalo or warriors.
  • Village and economy: Train takes people from other jobs when nobody is idle, "Train to 15%" and a warning past 20%, full stores shown on the tiles and in the report, a Welcome toast that shows once, a Kauhale for more homes, great works that can be raised to higher tiers, a Feast of plenty, gifts to allies, and cheaper first-tier discoveries.
  • Map: owners shown by color and border (rivals dashed), and rival realms drawn with a letter mark and a legend.
  • Cards and screens: event cards with four choices scroll as one card, the card title stays at the top while you scroll, courtiers give card-specific advice (22 cards got new lines; 5 one-choice cards show none), the title menu no longer flashes, and Settings fits small phones.
  • Endings: the medal is capped by goals met and the sheet says why, four lines of your chant show on the end sheet, and the titles no longer read "A chief remembered".
  • Text: American spelling throughout, no "Kingdom" before unification, "vassal" removed, and Hāʻena spelled with its kahakō.

5.5.22 (2026-11-24)

Google Play production 1230 at 100%, live 2026-11-24. App Store build 261124.1233 sent for review 2026-11-24. Closed testing 1230 the same day.

  • Advisers speak less often and only when it fits: a line about your past counsel shows only for a recent one, never twice in a row, and never praises advice that went badly. Cards with no real advice show none.
  • Cards now fit their season: the hōlua and the Makahiki heir card only in Makahiki, and the heiau repair only in winter. No courtier name is reused in a reign.
  • The Work and Army pages fit small phones: the idle count is no longer clipped, and a strip tells you when the Train buttons are below the fold.
  • Grayed-out buttons now say why (Welcome, raising a great work, gifts to a chief who already trusts you fully). Costs show as rounded figures, and what you see is what is charged.
  • Fixes: season reports show the real change in your stores, the Chronicle files entries under the right season, exile seasons agree on every screen, and the war sheet scales a chief's guard like everything else.
